QUEER LATINXS IN TECH

Logo, Identity & Branding

Monthly Gatherings in the SF Bay Area, Los Angeles, & San Diego for Queer LGBTQIA + GNC Latine/Latinx people in Tech. Queer Latinxs in Tech was founded in October 2021 with the mission of creating a space where Queerness and Latinidad intersect. As a proud participant in the SF Bay Area gatherings I occasionally assist in designs for some of the events.

PRIDE AT PINTEREST

In 2024, QLT (Queer Latinxs in Tech), in collaboration with the Latin@ and Pride@ ERGs at Pinterest, hosted a special Pride Month gathering. For the event, QLT took the opportunity to reimagine their logo, drawing inspiration from their core value of community building. As tech professionals, QLT members believe in the power of community to empower and uplift one another. Their events foster connection, shared experiences, and mutual support. The new logo’s building block and architectural forms symbolize QLT’s commitment to creating and strengthening community
