Monthly Gatherings in the SF Bay Area, Los Angeles, & San Diego for Queer LGBTQIA + GNC Latine/Latinx people in Tech. Queer Latinxs in Tech was founded in October 2021 with the mission of creating a space where Queerness and Latinidad intersect. As a proud participant in the SF Bay Area gatherings I occasionally assist in designs for some of the events.

CELEBRATING PRIDE
AT PINTEREST

In 2024, QLT (Queer Latinxs in Tech), in collaboration with the Latin@ and Pride@ ERGs at Pinterest, hosted a special gathering to celebrate Pride Month. For this event, QLT saw an opportunity to reimagine their logo.

In developing the new direction, I drew inspiration from QLT’s core value: community building. As professionals in the tech industry, QLT members believe in the power of community to empower and uplift one another. Their events create spaces to connect, share experiences, and support each others professional journeys.

The new logo’s forms are inspired by building blocks and architectural shapes, which are visual metaphors that reflect QLT’s commitment to constructing and strengthening community.
