ABOUT ALFONSO

Armed with a BFA from the California College of the Arts, I'm a graphic designer who is dedicated to creating cohesive visual narratives that reflect community identity and client vision. I am experienced in designing for local initiatives and collaborating closely with clients to translate ideas into meaningful, story-driven visuals that inspire connection and engagement.

My curiosity extends far beyond the screen and in my free time I'm busy dabbling in a wide array of mediums, from sculpting and painting to drawing and decorating. You could say I wear many hats (and yes, I do have a collection of hats I wear from every day), and I bring that same creative versatility to every project I take on.
